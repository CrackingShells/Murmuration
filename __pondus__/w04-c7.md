Rule: Q's reuse (3) is at least P's (3): met. Q has 0 overwrites: met. Q's median s3 (5,360) is at most P's (2,686): not met. Q is not proposed. P shows colgrep in 1 of 6 (at most 1): "search that folder once with colgrep `search`" is not followed at this size.

What the runs show beyond the rule:
- With the paragraph Q, which told workers to count and then, above about 100 files, to search with colgrep, 6 of 6 counted (`ls -1 | wc -l` printed 429) and none searched with colgrep; 5 of 6 then printed the first 100 names, the amount of listing the paragraph's "up to about 100" suggested, which is where Q's larger s3 comes from (about 3,800 characters of names in one call, against 30 to 50 names, about 1,300 to 2,100, in P).
- On t1 all 12 runs ran the fit script as is; its name sorts among the first files, so a listing cut at 30 would show it. On t2 no run opened the near fit (`collatz_longest_stopping_time.py`): P workers read `collatz_trajectory_print.py` (the word "trajectory" of the task), Q workers saw its first lines through `head -3 collatz_*` in 3 of 3 runs and still wrote a new plain loop; 0 of 6 adapted or reused it, against 5 adapted or edited of 9 (4 by copy, 1 in place) at 46 scripts in w04-c4 (a different set of runs, not a controlled comparison).
- The probe: one colgrep `search` at 429 scripts returned 5,136 characters, at 336 4,946 and 4,490: about the same as the 3,669 at 46; `ls -1` grew from 1,240 to 16,801. At 429 scripts a worker that prints the whole listing would spend 3.3 searches' worth of characters; none did (the listing was cut at 30 to 100 names).

## What this closes
It closes, at 429 scripts and n = 6 per arm: the reworded paragraph Q does not lower the search output or raise strict reuse over the shipped paragraph, and workers meet "search the folder" by `ls`, `ls | grep` and `head`, with or without a sentence that sends them to colgrep above 100 files. It does not close whether a paragraph that bans the full listing and prescribes one colgrep `search` first would be followed, or the near-fit case with a name that does not repeat a keyword of the task. Outside the scope: the explorer agent, subagents with the start note, opaque names at this size.
