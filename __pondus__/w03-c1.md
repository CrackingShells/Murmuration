## What this build decided (the verifier may escalate any of it)

1. Raise: new limit = max(limit, spend at the message) + x, minutes likewise (the PI's wording was "x more ... counted from that moment"; the alternative reading is spend + x). A raise on one budget leaves the other unchanged: raise both when both are spent.
2. A resume without `Budget:` or `Time:` lines restores nothing; spend and active time are cumulative. This replaces the baseline restart of the first hook (w02-c5), so a worker that is over budget stays over when a verifier or the PI questions it without a raise.
3. The notes use the check's own figures: whole percent is rounded and capped at 99 below 1.0; `t` has one decimal and `T` is printed with `%g`.
4. The start note cannot read the brief (the transcript does not exist at SubagentStart); a `PreToolUse` hook on `Agent` stores the brief's lines in `__statera__/.harness/pending/` for the SubagentStart that follows (each of the 6 e2e runs had one worker, and its start note said "from your brief" correctly). When two dispatches of one type with different figures are pending, the note does not guess and says the lines of the brief decide; after the reproducer's question 3 the first start marks the other pending record as ambiguous, so the order of the starts no longer matters (selftest, both orders; not run on the platform with the new file).
5. The floor is logged (`warn`, once per figure, at the dispatch), never applied.
6. The ceiling block stays on PostToolUse with a lock; PostToolBatch is silent in the batch it blocked.
