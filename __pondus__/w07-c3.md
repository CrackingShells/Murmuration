## What would be different if the claim were false

- x1: a `command` string that a shell splits differently from `shlex` (a placeholder with a space, a quote) would give a different argument list. Shown that the check can fail: with one argument of B changed in memory, both comparisons FAIL (`x1_form.txt`, second block).
- x2: a hook whose output depends on the shell (an environment variable, a `$`, a PATH the shell sets up) would differ. Shown that the check can fail: with B's guard rows made to start the budget script instead, 132 of 215 handler starts DIFF (`x2_process_mutant.txt`).
- x3: if exec form failed open, the guard's crash or a missing `python3` would let `echo w07c3-m1` run. It did not (below), and in both arms the control call (a `Read`, no guard) ran.
- x4: if B were not cheaper, per-pair medians would fall on either side of A. They fall on one side in 20 of 20 comparisons.

## What the saving is worth downstream (MEASURED, stated at its size)

- Hook time per session: the sum of the platform-recorded durations of every murmuration hook start in a session (about 36 starts) is lower in B by a median 3,021 ms (min 1,200, max 4,202, 10 of 10 pairs) (`x4_table.md`, last block). The hooks of a worker run concurrently with the model's next step only partly, so this is hook time, not wall time.
- Tool-call interval (tool_use to tool_result of the scripted `echo` calls, transcript timestamps, `toolgap_table.md`): per-pair median difference A minus B **54 ms, lower in B in 6 of 10 pairs, range -598 to +597 ms**. That interval holds the auto-mode classifier (about 0.5 to 1 s) and is far noisier than the 70 ms saving; these runs cannot see the saving in it.
- Session wall time (`x4_pairs_load.log`): A median 40.3 s, B median 43.6 s over the 10 pairs: B was not faster, model time varies by more than the hook saving; this says the saving is below what a 10-pair wall-time comparison resolves, not that it is absent.
- Size: 84 lines added, 42 changed in one file; no script changed; no process resident.
