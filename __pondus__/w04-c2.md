|:--|--:|--:|:--|:--|--:|
| uncapped A | 0 of 3 | 3 of 3 | 1.07 / 1.07 | 90, 90, 90 | 0 |
| uncapped B | 0 of 3 | 3 of 3 | 0.91 / 0.91 | 90, 71, 90 | 1 |
| capped A | 0 of 3 | 3 of 3 (2 in template shape) | 0.98 / 0.98 | 90, 68, 90 | 1 |
| capped B | 0 of 3 | 3 of 3 | 1.01 / 1.01 | 71, 90, 90 | 1 |

Every run made one hand-back call (overshoot after the first attempt: 0 in 12 of 12); every table row of every report was correct (items correct = items done in all 12). Decision by the pre-registered rule: "If A has denies in at most 1 of 3 runs, the problem did not reproduce: B is not proposed, reported as untested at this task." A had denies in 0 of 3 runs in both sets, so **B is not proposed on E1, and `agents/explorer.md` is unchanged.** The explorers of arm A wrote the report before handing back without the line, because their brief names it and their agent file already says "Your deliverable is one findings report".

Exploratory X (not a decision; written down before the runs in `00-addendum_v0.md`): the wave-3 shape, brief "list" (complete list in the hand-back), N = 150 (1.6 to 2 times the budget), same budget:

| capped XA (n = 3) | 3 of 3 | 3 of 3 | 0.99 | 1.20 | 0.21 |

| uncapped XA (n = 2) | 2 of 2 | 2 of 2 (0 of 2 in template shape) | 0.92 | 1.02 | 0.10 |

(XB was not repeated uncapped; the budget ran out.) The deny that wave 3 saw reproduces when the brief asks for the list in the hand-back: the explorer hands back at 0.86 to 1.07 of B without a report, is refused once, writes the report and hands back again, 0.10 to 0.23 of B later. With the line (capped XB) the 3 explorers wrote the report first (their reports are not in the template's shape: none has the front matter and the six headings, which the deny's reason names and the line does not). Read with the E1 rule as a reading aid only: A denies in at least 2 of 3, B in at most 1 of 3, B's report present in 3 of 3, B's median final fraction (1.00) not above A's (1.20): the rule would propose B on this task. It is exploratory and is not the decision.

What this closes: on briefs that already name the findings report as the deliverable, the one-line change has nothing to fix at N = 90 (the deny did not occur in 6 of 6 notes-on runs, 3 uncapped and 3 capped, of the unchanged arm). Outside that scope: briefs that ask for the work in the hand-back message, where the deny occurs and the line removed it in 3 of 3 capped runs (exploratory; whether to run E1 again on that brief, with a protocol `_v1`, is the principal investigator's decision).

Cost of the line: one sentence of 27 words in every explorer's prompt (ESTIMATE: about 35 tokens per explorer request, read from the cache).
