## What this closes
It closes, at this size, the question "does a worker that starts in a lab with dozens of scripts look into the folder and reuse what fits": yes for the author agent in 14 of 15 runs, by `ls` and `head`, and the paragraph is what makes it look (2 of 6 without it). It does not close whether the colgrep index earns its build: no worker needed it at 46 descriptive names, and the line that names it in the paragraph and the start note was followed 0 of 15 times. Outside the scope: a folder where `ls` is too long to read (hundreds of files) and the explorer agent.
