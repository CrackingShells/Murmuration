**Result against the registered rules.**
- Rule 1 (state survives): not met on Opus, met on haiku. Opus H run 1 (`op_H1`) answered Q2 (the instruction given at the session's start, verbatim) with "I can't quote it exactly", although its summary held the sentence and `user_words.md` held it; Opus H run 2, Opus B1 and Opus C answered all five. Haiku H 4 of 4 runs all five; B1 3 of 3; B0 2 of 3 strict (one run lost the in-flight names and next step from its summary and re-read 18,879 tokens, once from the session's own transcript file).
- Rule 2 (the hook adds something): not met. Items equal in all three haiku seeds; re-read tokens fewer for H in 1 of 3 seeds (4,229 against 13,998), equal in one (2,992), more in one (14,144 against 3,423). The hook did change the summary: the claims' labels were in 3 of 6 H summaries, 0 of 4 B1, 0 of 3 B0, and Q4 was correct everywhere because the PI re-reads small claim files.
- Rule 3 (re-reads at most 30,000 tokens on Opus H): met, 2,490 and 1,590 tokens (scratch files are small). Rule 4 (hook median under 500 ms, exit 0): met, six compactions 62-158 ms (median 88), ten cold runs on the real lab median 142 ms.
- Rule 5 (net at least USD 25): met as an ESTIMATE: USD 43-45 over the six PI sessions at 250k with R of 2,500-10,000 tokens, 39.5 at 20,000, 32.3 at 45,000 (`w08e_compaction_net.py@fc2d2ef` driven with the measured Opus summary of about 3,400 output tokens). Not a measured saving: in the measured 20-request sessions the compacted Opus runs cost USD 1.83-1.89 against 1.76 for the control; each later request is USD 0.016 cheaper, so the compaction pays back after about 28 requests.
- Rule 6 (setting): `autoCompactWindow` in a `--settings` file compacted a session end to end at 119,395 tokens (`thresholdSource=settings`); with `--project-config-root <main>` (the Desktop's way) the session loads the main checkout's `.claude/settings.json` and `settings.local.json` and not the worktree's; a `--settings` file changed between two turns was not re-read (window 980,000 at both). Writes of a project settings file were refused by the lab's guard (G3), so the project route and a running session's pickup of it are not tested.
- Workers: of the lab's 331 subagent transcripts, 22 (6.6%; 16.7% of the sonnet workers) pass 250k and 12.7% of worker requests are above it; ESTIMATE net USD -0.64 at 150k, +9.31 at 250k, +1.21 at 400k. A haiku subagent compacted twice kept its brief and a detail (4 of 4, as the control); the subagent's PreCompact payload carries no agent id, and the hook's stdout is ignored there. By the registered rule workers share the PI's threshold; they are not blocked.
