### independent result
Scripts: `__instrumenta__/w08_v3_blind_answers.py`, `w08_v3_compaction_cost.py`, `w08_v3_net_model.py`. Raw runs of mine: `/private/tmp/murmuration-w08/reproducer-w08-3/runs/` (not committed).

1. Scoring. I printed the final answers of all 17 counted runs under shuffled codes, with the planted truth, and scored the five questions by reading them (the answers' own wording leaks the arm in a few cases, so "blind" holds only in part). I find 15 of 17 runs correct on all five. Two fail Q2: the run that quoted a stale `user_words.md` entry and said it could not find the instruction (`hk_B0_1`, no brief), and the run that said "I can't quote it exactly ... the only copy I can reach is my summary" (`op_H1`, hook arm). I read `hk_B0_1`'s Q1 as substantively right (the ended workers sit in a "correction" paragraph). That matches the author's table on Q2-Q5; only the strict Q1 flag differs. The control runs are 4 of 4 on all five. So "kept what the PI needed" holds for B1 (Opus 1/1, haiku 3/3) and for H on haiku (4/4). It does not hold for Opus H (1 of 2), and the claim says so.
2. Fresh runs, seed 5 (the author used 1-3), same scripts and export of 9ac1855, window 150,000 by environment, default permission mode: haiku C, haiku B1, haiku H, Opus B1. All four answer all five by my reading and by the author's scorer. Re-read tokens: B1 haiku 2,343; H haiku 2,083; Opus B1 2,258 (the claim's 2.9k-14.0k range becomes 2.0k-14.0k). Hook time 62.9 ms. Cost as reported: Opus B1 USD 2.07, re-priced at Opus list 1.88 (author's Opus control 1.76, B1 1.83): a 20-request session does not repay. The Opus compaction request cost USD 0.119 (2,068 uncached, 116k read, 4.2k output), post-write 41,572 tokens at the 1 h price (all 1 h in the usage records) = USD 0.33, about 0.45 in all. The author's 0.100 / 41.8k / 0.44 trace to `debug.log` "Forked agent" usage and to the first assistant record after `compact_boundary` in `op_H1`, `op_H2`, `op_B1_1`, as I recomputed. One oddity: haiku H seed 5 compaction cost USD 0.287 (12.9k uncached input, 10.7k output) against 0.150 for B1 on that seed; haiku compaction cost ranges 0.10-0.29.
3. Net estimate. My own loop over explorer-w08-d's request tables reproduces 44.65 / 42.99 / 39.52 / 32.29 (T=250k, OUT 3,000, R 2.5k / 10k / 20k / 45k), and 31.27 at OUT 6,000, R 45k: the "USD 31-45" range. The same model gives 43.57 at 150k, so it does not rank thresholds.
4. Settings location. With `--project-config-root <folder>` in my check the folder's `settings.local.json` deny rule is applied (debug log "Applying permission update ... localSettings") and its `.claude/settings.json` path is probed; with cwd in an empty scratch folder and no flag neither happens. That backs "the flag chooses the folder whose project settings load". The worktree's and the main checkout's `settings.local.json` are byte-identical, so the deny rule alone cannot tell them apart; the watch-list paths in `m6_settings_location.txt` do. The key's effect was shown only through `--settings` (`thresholdSource=settings`, compaction at 119,395); the project-file route, the Desktop's passing of the flag (CITED) and a running session's re-read of project files are untested, which the claim states. The `--settings` file is not re-read mid-session (980,000 at both turns) is MEASURED but says nothing about project files, which are on the watch list.
5. Scope. The claim's scope already excludes a real PI at 250k and states n (Opus C 1, B1 1, H 2; with mine B1 is 2). Two things stay outside what the evidence carries: the transfer from a 25k-floor lab at 150k to a 91k-floor PI at 250k (a summarizer reading 250k may keep less; real files make R larger; the net model's R is 2.5k-45k, the measured 1.6-2.9k is the low end), and the choice of 283,000. What would show transfer: one Opus B1 run on a lab with the real PI's floor (about 90k of preamble and files) compacted at 250k, with Q2-Q5 scored as here.
