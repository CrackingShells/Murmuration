## The rule

A section `## decision: research-pi` (only the PI writes it; shell append, one writer per section) with `decision:` (one of `stands at <LABEL>`, `restated at <LABEL>`, `withdrawn`, `superseded by <id>`), `ref:` (`__statera__/wave-NN.md:LINE`) and, for `restated at`, `statement:`. The state shown: `<LABEL> (PI decided)`, `<LABEL> (PI decided: restated, not re-verified)`, `withdrawn (PI decided)`, `superseded by <id> (PI decided)`. The cap, enforced in `read_decision` (a refused decision is a warning and changes nothing):
- a label stronger than the author's is never applied (THEOREM > CHECKED > MEASURED > ESTIMATE > CONJECTURE);
- `stands at` and `restated at` apply only when a verdict above the decision asks for the PI (ESCALATE, OVERSTATED, LABEL TOO STRONG, or a dispute) and none above it is UNSUPPORTED or WRONG; on an unverified, CITED, verified, LABEL TOO WEAK or verifier-withdrawn claim only `withdrawn` or `superseded by` apply;
- over OVERSTATED or LABEL TOO STRONG, `stands at` must be strictly below the author's label; `restated at` may keep it, and then the state says "not re-verified";
- `ref:` must be a wave note that exists and names the claim id (a line that does not name it is a warning, because line numbers drift); the writer name must be `research-pi`; one `decision:` line.
A verdict section written after the decision counts: state is computed from the verdicts after it alone, at the label the decision left, so SUPPORTED shows the bare label again, OVERSTATED, ESCALATE and UNSUPPORTED show their usual states. The last decision that can be applied stands.

## What this does not close

Closed: that `lab_status.py` can take a PI decision section without changing any claim that has none, and that it refuses to show a label above the author's or above a verifier's UNSUPPORTED/WRONG. Not closed: (a) a `restated at <head label>` over OVERSTATED is allowed, because the 2 real cases (w02-c7, w02-c8) were restated in place at the same label on the verifier's own figures; the tool cannot read whether `statement:` matches the verifier's result, it only says "not re-verified"; a stricter rule (the label must drop on OVERSTATED) would refuse those 2; (b) the state `superseded by <id>` from a `supersedes:` line hides a decision on a superseded claim (the 4 above), the `[PI decision: ref]` mark stays; (c) `agents/research-pi.md` and `references/waves.md` do not yet tell the PI to write the section (only `SKILL.md` does); (d) the 10 distinct wave-note lines cited as `ref:` are line numbers in notes that the PI may still edit; the tool warns when the line stops naming the claim.
