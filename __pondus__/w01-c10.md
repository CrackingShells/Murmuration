## Risk found by the verifier's replay (stated here, not solved)
The transcript's cwd follows the Bash tool: in ab96c5ed, 390 records say the worktree and 49 (lines 66-144) say the main checkout, right after a Bash call that `cd`'d there; the headless payload `cwd` follows it too (case 6). Under a cwd-first rule, a human prompt typed while an agent's shell sits in another checkout (or another repository that has a `__statera__/`) goes to THAT lab. Whether the Desktop payload holds the live shell cwd or the session cwd is unknown. Options for the PI, not tested: take the session's first cwd (the first transcript record) instead; or accept the CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR lab when it exists and use the cwd lookup only when the two checkouts are worktrees of the same repository (`git rev-parse --git-common-dir` equal), which removes the other-repository case but not the other-checkout case.
