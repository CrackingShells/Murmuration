### The change

`current_label` collects every line of a verifier's section that the existing `verdict:` pattern matches and takes the last. When there are two or more and no `changed_by:` line occurs below the second-to-last one, it adds the warning "verdict section '<name>' has N `verdict:` lines and no `changed_by:` line below the earlier one; the last counts". The docstring line "whose first `verdict:` line counts" now says last and one clause says how a revision looks. `skills/research-loop/SKILL.md`: "The first line of the section that starts with `verdict:` is the verdict" became "The last line ... is the verdict (a revision adds a new `verdict:` line and a `changed_by:` line below the earlier one)".
