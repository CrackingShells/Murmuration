## Decisions these support, by the pre-registered rules

- The lean workers and the findings template land (rule met in B and B2).
- The hand-back saver lands (every hand-back saved in B–B4).
- The deny-once check is not exercised in any arm; it stands on w02-c5's probe cells.
- The budget hook lands as insurance with a stated limit. It keeps deliverables under pressure (B4, 2 of 2), but it checks only between tool calls, so a single request can overshoot by about one request's cost. The overshoot rule (≤ 1.2) fails at B = 0.05 (0 of 2) and is undecided at real budgets, where nothing fired.
- The README's ceiling advice (w02-c7) is consistent with B2–B4 but was not needed by them.

## What would differ if the claim were false

If the candidate changes broke the research, a candidate arm's synthesis would be wrong against the brute force, or a deliverable would be missing. If the context saving came from somewhere other than the change, B's workers would start as large as A's. Neither holds. The causal reading of the scope trend is the weakest part, and it is labelled CONJECTURE in the title.
