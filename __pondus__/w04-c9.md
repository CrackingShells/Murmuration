- By the pre-registered E2 rule the notes-on explorers (arm A, n = 3, 90 files, brief names the report) read as compliance: median first hand-back 1.07 B uncapped and 0.98 B capped (hook estimate, ESTIMATE of USD), items per USD 263.8 against 268.8 for arm C uncapped, accuracy 100% in all runs. The wave-3 driver (`e2e_drive.py`, default `--cap 2.5`) opened the platform's spend channel (`budget_usd` attachment) as my first capped runs did; the wave-3 transcripts were not checked for it.
- Bases: n1, n5 and all "fraction" figures are the hook-estimate basis; "1.6 and 1.9 times B" of w04-c3's uncapped XC runs were the platform's sonnet cost over B (1.61, 1.95; on the hook basis 1.45 and 1.83). The pooled "3 of 12 against 0 of 6" was descriptive and not used in any decision; the rule's N is arm A alone (capped 1 of 3, uncapped 0 of 3 stopped short).

Rule, as pre-registered. "The notes are what stops the worker if C runs past 1.3 B in at least 2 of 3 runs [3 of 3: yes] while N hands back between 0.9 and 1.2 B in at least 2 of 3 [3 of 3: yes]": **holds** (n = 3, one task, sonnet). Anxiety needs N's median n1 below 0.9 (it is 1.04) with items per USD or accuracy more than 15% below C's (N's items per USD median is 15% above C's; accuracy equal), or a budget reason with at least 10% left in 2 of 3 runs (unused 0.03, 0, 0): **not met on any clause**. E2's compliance clause 1 (median n1 at least 0.9) is met, and this time the control ran 1.5 B or more, so it is not met by arm C's behaviour: without notes the worker did not stop. Caveats that bear on the reading: N delivered fewer items than C in 2 of 3 runs (33 and 56 of 70 against 70), the price of stopping at B; one N run stopped at 33 files at 0.97 B with 37 unread, a lower yield per USD (115.7) than C's best, so the early stop is costly when the per-call cost is high; and the 15% gap in items per USD in N's favour is within what n = 3 can bear (range of N: 115.7 to 204.3).

What this closes: at USD 0.30 and a 70-file task of about 1.5 B, with sonnet explorers and a brief that names the report, the notes stopped the workers near the budget and no worker without notes stopped, and the stops did not leave budget unused or deliver less per USD than the notes-off arm. Not covered: tasks that fit the budget with room; opus workers; a worker with a spend channel other than the hook's (the platform's `budget_usd` attachment, shown by the capped runs of w04-c3, also made workers stop); the effect of the notes on work quality beyond item correctness.
