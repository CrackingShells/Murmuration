ESTIMATE, same method as w05-c3 (`w05c3_desktop_projection.py` on the wave-4 snapshot): D removes 122,882 of 186,530 loaded characters, which is 25.9k to 42.0k tokens (characters times 0.211, measured, to 0.342, explorer-w05-b's fit); B removed 127,219 (26.8k to 43.5k), so D gives up 4,337 characters (about 1k to 1.5k tokens) against B. Deferred names: 213 to 210 (68 characters), against B's 213 to 37; so D keeps 173 more deferred names (about 7.9k characters, 1.7k to 2.7k tokens) than B, among them the user's GitHub, file-storage and calendar connectors. Wave-4 PI main thread: about 7.5% to 12.2% of its list price by explorer-w05-b's formula (21.7% for the base context times the removed share), against 7.8% to 12.7% for B.
