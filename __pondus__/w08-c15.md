# w08-c15: `-`-prefixed short-option clusters containing `c` (after options such as `-l`, `-e`, `-x`, `-i`, `-o name`) are read as `-c` by the guard for sh, bash, zsh, dash and ksh in Python and Rust, closing what the plain form `<shell> -c '<script>'` denies (0 of 20,282 verifier variants allowed, against 8,098 on the base: 6,882 of 18,202 claude starts and 1,216 of 2,080 settings writes), with nothing loosened; `+c`/`+lc` clusters and the zsh `-O` and ksh `-o` misreadings still pass

label: MEASURED
scope: Same change and same evidence as w08-c14 (commit 87abdcb of branch w08-c14-shellc): Bash commands whose program, after wrappers, is sh, bash, zsh, dash or ksh, with a `-`-prefixed letter cluster containing `c`, options before it (`-l -e -x -i`, `-o name`, `-O name`, long options, `--rcfile F`) and after it (`-c -x`, `-c --`, `-lc -`), in 8 spellings, 14 wrappers, nesting 1-4, and the author's 36,280 cases.
