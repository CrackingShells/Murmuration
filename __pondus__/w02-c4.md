## What would be different if the claim were false

- If the template and the skill section did not steer the report, the pre-change plugin would match as well as the new one: it does not. With the same brief, old a and b wrote no file at the convention path (0 of 2); new wrote it 6 of 6.
- If the report did not follow the template, `measure.py` would show other headings or leftover placeholders or the template's comment line: new 6 of 6 have the six headings in order (`Result`, `Table`, `Worst-fitting case`, `Not covered`, `Steering questions`, `Pointers`), the six front-matter keys, no comment line, no `<...>` placeholders.
- If removing `Skill` also lost the preload, `research_loop_preloaded` would be false and the report shape would fail: it is true 6 of 6 (and 3 of 3 for the smoke starts of author, reproducer, proof-adversary).
- If `Skill` were not what carries the listing, the control would not differ: the control carries a 29,919-character `skill_listing` attachment and starts at 23,408 / 23,408 / 23,442 tokens; new carries no listing attachment and starts at 12,280 to 12,341.

## Cost of the change

- Tokens saved per worker start: about 11,100 (23,408 to 12,280, 47%), from the 29,919-character listing; against the pre-change plugin about 10,550, because the new skill text adds about 570 tokens (22,836 to 23,408 with `Skill` held fixed). MEASURED. Per-run USD saving is not claimed; the runs differ in turns.
- Words added to shipped files: 444 (4,263 to 4,707 by `wc -w` over `agents/` and `skills/research-loop/`): template 151, `briefs.md` 133, `SKILL.md` 127, `explorer.md` 24, `waves.md` 6, other three agent files 1 each. `SKILL.md` is preloaded in every worker.
- Lost by removing `Skill`: a worker can no longer load a skill on demand (for example another plugin's `writing-reports`). In these runs no worker called `Skill` (0 of 11 runs), and the skill the workers need arrives by `skills:`. The templates are carried by `research-loop`, so no second skill is needed.
- `maxTurns` went from 60 to 150 for the explorer and was added at 150 to the other three, as the PI's brief instructed; this claim has no evidence that 150 is the right value. The runs took 5 to 10 assistant turns, no run was stopped by the limit, and nothing here shows it is honoured. What the runs do show is that the frontmatter of the four files loads (the `tools:` change took effect). The explorer's limit is now 2.5 times its old one, and the only brake in the shipped text besides it is the single budget note.

## With the worktree's hooks loaded (added after the verifier's question)

`WITH_HOOKS=1 bash setup.sh` (or `quick.sh`) copies the worktree's `hooks/` (author-w02-3's uncommitted `SubagentStart`, `PreToolUse`, `PostToolUse`, `SubagentStop` entries) into the new and ctl copies. Result in `result_with_hooks.jsonl`, USD 0.67: first-turn context is unchanged (new 12,313 to 12,320 tokens, ctl 23,441, old 22,867), the 29,919-character listing is absent for new, `research-loop` is preloaded, and all five new runs wrote a report at the convention path. The user's own global `SubagentStart` hooks inject their policies in every run, with or without the plugin hooks, so they are inside every number above; the plugin's `budget.py` injected nothing I could see in these runs. The template shape held in 3 of 5 runs with hooks (new_a and new_d did not read the template and wrote their own headings, without front matter), against 7 of 7 without hooks. I do not know whether the hooks cause this; n is small and I did not find a mechanism in the injected contexts. So "6 of 6 in the template's shape" holds without hooks, and is 3 of 5 with them. MEASURED. Landing order: the budget sentence in `briefs.md` and the "hands back" line in `waves.md` describe the harness note of w02-c3, so they are only true once that hook stands.

## Limits

- One brief, one dataset, sonnet explorers only; `n` is 6 and 3, so 6 of 6 leaves a failure rate of up to about 46% inside a 95% interval (Clopper-Pearson upper bound).
- The brief names the report; whether a long, many-item brief still ends in a report is open (S-w02-explorer-w02-b-3).
- The budget sentence was in every brief; whether the workers act on the single budget note was not tested here.
