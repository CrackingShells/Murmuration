## Result

MEASURED: of the PI's USD 144.47, 56% (81.15) is cache reads of context already there, 18% (26.02) is fresh cache writes, 10% (14.19) is re-writes of context that was already there but not read, 16% (23.11) is billed output; uncached input is USD 0.01. Every cache write of the PI is 1-hour (5.0 M tokens at the 1-hour rate, 0.00 M at the 5-minute rate) [source: `__reports__/wave-08/explorer-w08-d/tables.md` section 1].

ESTIMATE (source model below): by what put the tokens there, the PI's own words are the largest family, 44% (USD 63.84): billed output 23.11, plus what that output costs while it sits in the context (own thinking carried 18.76, own tool calls 9.00, dispatch briefs 6.32, Write/Edit content 3.47, text 2.80). Injected text and the prefix are 22% (31.79; the skill listing alone 14.78, 10.2%), tool results 17.5% (25.29), workers' hand-backs, task notifications and child-session notes 12.2% (17.68), the one real compaction's context 3.9% (5.64), the user's messages 0.2% (0.24). The ten costliest single items are USD 16.78 together (11.6%), and eight of them are the system prefix, the post-compaction context or the skill listing; the costliest document read is a 33,409-character report read at request 9 of s1, USD 0.80.

MEASURED, pauses: 69 of 1,116 requests (6.2%) follow a gap over 5 minutes, 7 (0.6%) a gap over 1 hour. 61 of the 62 gaps between 5 minutes and 1 hour were cache hits (1-hour cache). 7 requests missed (cache read under half of the previous context) and re-wrote USD 14.17 of context (9.8% of PI spend; USD 13.82 over what a read would have cost); 6 of the 7 gaps over 1 hour missed, the seventh was a hit after 87 minutes, and one miss (s1 request 290) came 8 minutes after a full re-write.

ESTIMATE, levers (formulas in "Table" and in `tables.md` section 5; not additive, since compaction shrinks the base the others act on): compaction when the context passes 200-300k tokens, USD 35-46 net (24-32%); delegating the orientation reads, USD 8.9 (6.1%); hand-backs capped at 1,000-3,000 characters, USD 6.0-10.3 (4.1-7.1%); reading documents by section, USD 3.1-4.6 (2.1-3.2%); fewer requests per dispatch batch, USD 0.75 (0.5%) as the strict count, at most USD 12 as an upper bound; compacting right before a pause that turns out to exceed an hour, USD 5.3 (3.6%): the four pauses with a context over 245k tokens give USD 6.1, the three with 117k-166k give about zero or less.

## Worst-fitting case

The story that fits best: the PI's money is its own words and the context it carries, not any single read; compaction beats every habit change.

Fits worst, in four parts.

1. The levers the brief names are small next to the compaction estimate, and the compaction estimate is the one with the least evidence. Reading by section (USD 3.1-4.6), delegating orientation (8.9), shorter hand-backs (6.0-10.3) and fewer dispatch requests (0.75) are each 0.5-7% of PI spend. The compaction estimate (24-32%) assumes the PI loses nothing by compacting and, in its second variant, re-reads 45k tokens; only one real compaction exists (s1 request 276) and the compacted context was then re-written after a 71-minute pause and again 8 minutes later (puzzle 1). s1 alone gives USD 25.11 of the 46.42 at trigger 200k; without s1 the sessions give USD 21.31 (200k) and 17.25 (300k). [source: `tables.md` section 5(d)]
2. "Orientation reads" are a small share in the sessions where the PI reads with `Read`: the PI mostly looks at files through `cat`/`sed`/`head` in Bash (80 items, USD 5.26) and `Read` of documents shows 15 items (USD 1.88); orientation items to the first dispatch cost USD 0.47-2.36 per session over their lives. The synthesis's "first turn 21-32% of spend" (row 29) fits the prefix-heavy waves (s2 43.4%, s3 43.2% of the session's USD come from items that entered before the first dispatch) and fits worst s1 (15.1%) and s4 (24.4%), where later conversation dominates. The share of s6 (70%) is of a 29-request session and will fall. [source: `tables.md` section 2c]
3. The typical request does not fit the pause story: the median gap is 5.4 s, 93.8% of requests follow a gap under 5 minutes, and 61 of 62 pauses of 5 minutes to 1 hour were hits; only 7 requests (USD 14.17, 9.8%) were misses, six after more than an hour of the user being away (a user message ended five). The 1-hour tier looks well chosen: USD 15.08 extra in write price against USD 119.07 of counterfactual re-writes. [source: `tables.md` section 4]
4. The attribution is a model, and its weakest class is the largest single bucket after output: "own thinking carried" (USD 18.76) is inferred from a residual, not read from text. Correlation with billed output is 0.85-0.94 but s5's residual (180k tokens) exceeds billed output minus visible text (126k) by 54k, which per-step reminders and framing could explain, or not. If the thinking blocks were dropped from the context between turns, this bucket would be framing and reminders, and the "own words" family would fall from 44.2% to 31.2% (63.84 - 18.76 = 45.08 of 144.47). [source: `tables.md` section 2b]

- Model of the attribution: context of request k = input + cache read + cache write tokens; tokens at positions below `cache_read` are read, the rest are written (at the blended 5-minute/1-hour price) or input; the positions below the previous request's context that were not read are re-writes.
