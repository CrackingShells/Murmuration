Do these six problems in order, one per turn. For each one: reason about it carefully and privately, without code and without any written text, then make exactly one Bash call whose command is `echo <your final answer>` (nothing else in the command), and write no text before or after the call. After the sixth call, reply with the single word `done`.
1. How many integers from 1 to 1000 are divisible by 3 or by 5 but not by 15?
2. How many ways can 8 distinct books be arranged on a shelf so that two particular books are never adjacent?
3. What is the remainder when 7^2026 is divided by 13?
4. How many positive divisors does 2520 have, and how many of them are even?
5. In how many ways can you make change for 100 cents with pennies, nickels, dimes and quarters?
6. What is the sum of the digits of 2^64?
