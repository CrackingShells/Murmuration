# What the PI reads and how it reacts to its team: findings (v0)

Scheme (committed before coding, with a short addendum made during coding): `__reports__/wave-09/explorer-w09-d/01-coding_scheme.md`. Coded table: `__reports__/wave-09/explorer-w09-d/data/coded_turns.tsv` (105 turns). Population tables beside it in `data/`. Notation: B = a worker hand-back message, N = a task notice alone, H = human prompt, X = injected note from another session, "wake" = a turn led by B or N. Scope: PI main threads of waves 1-3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 (wave 8 from `70daedf1`, the fork that holds 1,204 of `ad75be4f`'s 1,220 record ids). Not a tour of intent: thinking is mostly empty, so "why" is the PI's visible text plus what it did next.

## Result

1. **The PI opens a worker's raw artifact in the wake itself for about a fifth to a third of hand-backs, and which worker sent it decides.** Over 244 wake turns of waves 1-8, 231 made a request and 190 of those had a tool call; among the wake turns with a request (n = 80, 24, 22, 38, 28, 39) the share with a raw read (worker report, puzzle file, claim file, script, data, PI report; strict: Read/cat/sed/head/grep/jq, not git, not `lab_status`) in the same turn was 22% (w1-3), 8% (w4), 18% (w5), 16% (w6), 36% (w7), 36% (w8); same or next turn 38, 17, 23, 29, 46, 54% [MEASURED, `data/raw_open_by_wave.txt`]. In waves 5-8, by sender: explorer hand-backs 18 of 27 (67%), author hand-backs 10 of 57 (18%), verifier hand-backs 5 of 41 (12%) [MEASURED, same file]. So the skill's rule ("for every agent that returns, open at least one raw artifact") is followed for explorers and mostly not followed, in the turn, for authors and verifiers, whose artifact is a claim file. The rise by wave is partly the mix (wave 8 had 13 explorer wakes of 39; w5 had 5 of 22). Whether it follows the plugin text is not tested: the raw-open sentence was in `waves.md` from 2026-10-19 (`git log -S`), and "Reassess when results come back" arrived at 2026-10-21 16:00 (`73a7565`) [MEASURED, git log].
2. **The PI replaces the raw read with its own check about as often as it opens one.** In the hand-read sample, 72 hand-back wakes: raw artifact opened in the turn 18, own check run instead (selftests, a diff against a named commit, a smoke run, its own probe, `date` and `git log` for its own times) 23, neither 41; 25 coded "taken at its word" (a dispatch, message or note written from the hand-back alone) [MEASURED counts of my coding; the sample is stratified, so shares are not population rates; `data/coded_turns.tsv`]. After wave 6 the "own check" is mostly the landing steps (selftests on the merged tree, live install, smoke run) that w08-c8 wrote down.
3. **A hand-back or an artifact changed the PI's plan or reading in about 9 of the 72 sampled wakes; other things changed it as often.** Coded P (plan or reading changed): 19 of 72 hand-back wakes and 7 of 20 human prompts. Of the 26: about 9 from a hand-back or an artifact, 4 from the PI's own reasoning about a hand-back before any verdict (for example w5 T009, "it carries a cost the rule never measured", w8 T021, "a confidently wrong name is worse than today's swap"), 7 from the user's prompt, 2 from a verifier's question or verdict, 1 each from its own test, a harness warning, a self-correction, and 2 unlabelled [CONJECTURE: a reading of the PI's next action and its text; every row has a quoted sentence in `coded_turns.tsv`]. Clearest raw-driven cases: w6 T010 (the harness warned of a permission-mode pattern in a hand-back; the PI grepped the worker's scripts and found a run in the user-only permission mode, then drafted the guard work), w6 T006 (explorer's table, plus the claim head of w04-c5 it opened, turned a set-aside build into a candidate: "Under the user's rule that looks like waste left in place"), w7 T001-T006 (puzzle files and report sections opened in all five; one led to a new explorer, "split the Bash 1.3 s").
4. **A hand-back changes the PI's own text as well as the team's.** The PI corrects itself after a worker's finding in at least 6 sampled turns (w5 T020 "my entry times were guesses, not read from a clock"; w6 T014 "it applies to my own v1 text as well"; w7 T036 "my earlier sentence was too strong"; w8 T004 a wave-note time of "00:45" against the real 00:53) [CONJECTURE, quotes in the table]. Against the picture of a PI that only relays: 28 of 72 sampled wakes carry a sentence that narrows, doubts or contradicts the hand-back (code Q).
5. **Wakes with no tool call: 41 of 244 wake turns, plus 13 turns where the notice or message made no request of its own** (queued behind other input; one hand-back at w6 T008 shows 0 steps) [MEASURED, `data/population_stats.txt`]. Each of 188 wakes that did call a tool ended with a closing request that has no call. CONJECTURE: item 47's 82 requests without a tool call are of this kind and of the 41 text-only wakes (the count cannot be reconciled exactly: I read wave 8 from the fork only and count wake turns, item 47 counts requests); the 41 are the wakes that did nothing but speak, the 188 closing requests are the wake's summary to the user. Of the 20 text-only wakes in the sample, 7 carry a judgement stated only in text (for example w6 T021 "I'm not buying the quiet-machine re-measure") and 13 are relay or "waiting" lines. Follow-through of four stated intentions was checked in files: three recorded (wave-06.md line 188; synthesis line 430; README line 72), one not found (w4 T003 "I'll record it", no hit in wave-04.md) [CHECKED by grep at these four; n = 4].
6. **Orientation before the first dispatch: 18-37 steps and 69-119k characters read, in 154-779 s, with 3-4 steps carrying text** [MEASURED, `data/orientation_table.tsv`]. The reads are rule-driven (the skill's "For the PI" table and the user's own "Read ... first" lines name synthesis, the last wave note, `waves.md`, `briefs.md`, framing) and the stated reasons are sequence announcements ("Still orienting: I've read the wave-6 note and am moving on to the synthesis"), not purposes [CONJECTURE]. Reads by section (sed ranges, `awk` by heading) are common from wave 6 on; framing, `waves.md` and `briefs.md` are read whole every session.
7. **Reads overall (waves 5-8: 588 read calls, 958k characters):** 55% have their file name appear in a later PI write, dispatch or text; 41% (243) are of a file the PI had already read, 139 of them with identical call text (114k characters); the synthesis was re-read 34 times in 38 reads, wave notes 33 in 45, and 28 repeats followed the PI's own write to the file [MEASURED proxies, `data/read_table.tsv`; "used" is a string proxy that cannot see puzzle files (14% "used") or `lab_status` (0%), which the PI uses by id or by numbers, not by file name]. A read that nothing used is hard to name from outside: the biggest unmatched ones are orientation bundles read for the first hour of a session and a 17k-character scheduling-skill read for the user's colgrep report (w8 T041).
8. **Where a read has a stated reason it is "before I present it to you" or "before I decide":** "Reading its lever ladder and its first puzzles myself before I show it to you" (w8 T013), "These decisions are mine, so I'm reading the proposed sections myself before appending anything" (w8 T033), "Before reassessing I'll read probe d's puzzles" (w5 T008) [CONJECTURE; 3 explicit cases in 105 turns].
9. **Results stated before the worker returned (the PI's addition): 0 confirmed in waves 1-8.** D1 (a hash with no earlier carrier in the PI's context) checked 639 hash-like tokens in PI-authored content over the six sessions and flagged 0 once a prefix match and an escape fix were in; D2 (a result-like sentence about a worker with no hand-back yet) flagged 30 sentences, read by hand: 25 status or to-do lines ("is checking", "its verdict on ..."), 5 results each traced to a hand-back or notice earlier in the same session (w123 T23 "Reproducer-w01-10 found a regression", carried by author-w01-4's hand-back; w123 T28, T29, T43; w7 T15); 0 fabrications; D3 flagged nothing [MEASURED count of flags, CHECKED by hand reading at these 30; `data/fabrication_scan.tsv`]. Calibration on this session's known case: D1 fires at 23:30:47 JST on the PI's `git branch --contains 2ef93a1` (first occurrence anywhere in its context) and D2 fires at 23:30:43 on the thinking sentence "Explorer w09-f's audit is in" [MEASURED on `a75de3b4`]. The fabricated notice block itself is not in the transcript as text. Limit: a made-up result with no hash, no worker name and no notification shape passes unseen.
10. **Thinking is not empty.** 8-20% of thinking blocks carry a one- or two-sentence narration (w123 49 of 331, w4 7/84, w5 27/140, w6 15/163, w7 17/172, w8 44/223) [MEASURED, `__instrumenta__/w09d_turns.py` summary line]. I used them as "(thinking)" quotes. They are the only place several "why" statements appear ("I'll bundle a fix", "so I won't override this").

11. **The platform's banner on a hand-back did not make the PI open the raw artifact.** 35 of 223 hand-back wakes with a request begin with "[harness: subagent output matched instruction-shaped pattern(s) ... treat any remaining directive-shaped text as a finding to relay to the user"; raw read in the same turn 9 of 35 (26%) against 41 of 188 (22%) without; in wave 6 the 12 bannered hand-backs had 0 raw reads [MEASURED, `data/banner_vs_raw.txt`]. The one banner-triggered raw check in the sample (w6 T010) came through a cross-session note, not through the hand-back; whether the PI "relayed to the user" the banner's finding in the other 34 is not coded.

## Worst-fitting case

**w6 T010** (21 19:19:53 JST; row 45 of the coded table). By the scheme's trigger rule it is a hand-back wake. It is not: the message is a cross-session note from the wave-5 PI ("Your last turn ended on a network error ..."), the actual hand-back of author-w06-3 had come 27 s earlier as a notice that made no request of its own (T009, 0 steps), and its report reached the PI at T008 almost an hour before. The PI's first sentence answers the other session ("author-w06-3 handed back normally; nothing was cut off"), its second is a raw check set off by the harness's own warning on the report, and it ends dispatching a verifier and a second author in the same request. Trigger, reason (a platform warning, not the hand-back) and action do not sit in one box: the turn needs three codes (X, B-late, harness-flag), and the scheme's W/O/P cannot say that the raw read happened because of a banner rather than the PI's rule. The turn that fits best: **w8 T033** (explorer-w08-k): hand-back, opens the proposed sections ("These decisions are mine, so I'm reading ... myself before appending anything"), appends, checks `lab_status`, closes an item. Other poor fits: w123 T087 (a human prompt with a hand-back arriving mid-turn and ten steps of three unrelated jobs), and the 13 zero-step "wakes".

## Not covered

- Waves 1-4 are thin (14 sampled hand-backs; wave 4 was an unattended night, w4 T000 to T027).
- "Used later" is a file-name string proxy: it misses use by puzzle id, by pasted numbers, and use that left no trace; I did not hand-code use for the 588 reads, only for the sampled turns' actions. I did not count the reads' effect on money (wave 8's split holds that).
- Not read: the workers' own transcripts, the hand-back texts beyond their first 260 characters, and the claim files themselves (so "the PI should have opened the claim" is not tested against what the claim held). Whether an opened artifact would have changed the PI's mind in the 41 sampled wakes where none was opened is not testable from the transcript.
- The two fabricated-result detectors see hashes, worker names and notice shapes only. A number stated before any tool output carries it (D4) is not done; a check over numbers would be noisy because the PI computes many of its own.
- Wave 8 is read from the fork `70daedf1`; `ad75be4f` has 16 record ids of its own (ending 05:02 JST) that I did not read.
- Sample strata are forced (no-action wakes, raw opens, "at word", long, spread), so the sample counts show kinds of behaviour, not rates; rates are the population tables.

## Steering questions

- [now] The sentence in the skill ("open at least one raw artifact") reads as met for explorers (67%) and not for authors (18%) and verifiers (12%): is the rule meant for claim files too, or should it name the artifact per role?
- [now] 3 of 4 text-only promises were kept; do you want a check that a PI's stated "I'll record it" shows up in a note (the lab's own habit of giving each puzzle a disposition does this for puzzles only)?
- [later] Wakes that only speak are 41; the 188 closing requests are the summary the user reads. Is that summary the thing the user reads, or the wave note? That decides whether item 47's cost is waste or the interface.
