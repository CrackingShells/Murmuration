## What would differ if the claim were false, and the case that shows it

The hunch has two parts: (H1) a resume re-writes the whole context to the cache; (H2) the work sits stranded for minutes until the PI notices. If H1 held for b, c, d, the first turn after the resume would show cache_creation close to its whole context (the cache-write share of context near 1). If H2 held, the gap from the stop to the resume would be minutes. The instrument can see both: in the same tables, 5 of 12 resumes (all with gaps of 425 s or more, in the genesis and design sessions) have a cache-write share of 0.81 to 0.87, so a cold resume is visible when it happens. For b, c, d the share is 0.004 to 0.006. H1 is false for these three, and H2 is false by two orders of magnitude.
