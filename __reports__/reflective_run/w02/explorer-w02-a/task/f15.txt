The Sundrel station was founded in 1730 by Tomas Lund and stands 123 metres above the valley floor. Its population is about 37974 people, most of whom work in glass blowing. A local saying holds that the grey wind arrives every 28 days, and the council records this in a ledger kept since 1798.
