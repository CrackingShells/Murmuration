The Orrisong station was founded in 1954 by Ada Verrin and stands 308 metres above the valley floor. Its population is about 60095 people, most of whom work in salt harvesting. A local saying holds that the grey wind arrives every 33 days, and the council records this in a ledger kept since 2044.
