The Lorrimar station was founded in 1737 by Bram Haldane and stands 72 metres above the valley floor. Its population is about 67400 people, most of whom work in glass blowing. A local saying holds that the grey wind arrives every 12 days, and the council records this in a ledger kept since 1804.
