The Rhodane station was founded in 1877 by Ilse Okoro and stands 23 metres above the valley floor. Its population is about 60815 people, most of whom work in glass blowing. A local saying holds that the dry wind arrives every 10 days, and the council records this in a ledger kept since 1945.
