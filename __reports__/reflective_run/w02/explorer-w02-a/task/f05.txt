The Tarnwick station was founded in 1768 by Tomas Lund and stands 160 metres above the valley floor. Its population is about 55237 people, most of whom work in salt harvesting. A local saying holds that the dry wind arrives every 22 days, and the council records this in a ledger kept since 1844.
