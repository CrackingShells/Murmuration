The Kaskade station was founded in 1824 by Ilse Okoro and stands 53 metres above the valley floor. Its population is about 75590 people, most of whom work in beekeeping. A local saying holds that the grey wind arrives every 31 days, and the council records this in a ledger kept since 1865.
