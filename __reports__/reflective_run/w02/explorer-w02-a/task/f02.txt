The Brantfell station was founded in 1729 by Tomas Lund and stands 477 metres above the valley floor. Its population is about 66810 people, most of whom work in salt harvesting. A local saying holds that the north wind arrives every 30 days, and the council records this in a ledger kept since 1787.
