The Quillmoor station was founded in 1865 by Ada Verrin and stands 89 metres above the valley floor. Its population is about 52050 people, most of whom work in salt harvesting. A local saying holds that the dry wind arrives every 9 days, and the council records this in a ledger kept since 1916.
