The Ostrava-9 station was founded in 1735 by Bram Haldane and stands 135 metres above the valley floor. Its population is about 12189 people, most of whom work in salt harvesting. A local saying holds that the dry wind arrives every 10 days, and the council records this in a ledger kept since 1768.
