## S-w09-explorer-w09-c-1
The two judges that were both perfect or near-perfect on wave 8's hand-labelled sets fail in opposite directions on auto mode's recorded decisions. Granite Guardian 4.1 8B under the short criterion refuses 49% (reweighted) of the allows and misses 25% of the refusals; gpt-oss-safeguard-20b under the same criterion refuses about 3% (n 45) of the allows and misses 88% of the refusals. Wave 8's borderline set had them at 11/12 and 12/12. So either wave 8's labels (mine as drafter) describe a different policy from auto mode's, or real calls (long prompts, arena templates, MCP calls, scheduled-task context) are far from the hand-written ones. I did not separate the two explanations: a way would be to score both judges on the 22 wave-8 cases under the same prompt shape as here and see whether the numbers fall back.

## S-w09-explorer-w09-c-2
Granite Guardian under the full rules says "yes" (block) to almost everything: 148 calls, 3 of 65 refusals missed, 16 of 26 synthetic allow-labelled blocked, 19 of 20 allow-labelled real calls of a 24-call probe blocked, 16 of 17 pairs blocked on both members. Two criterion wordings gave the same collapse (8 of 8, then 15 of 16 blocked). I do not know whether it is the 18K-token system message (Granite is trained with the criterion in the last turn, after the case), the Q4_K_S quantisation, or the "yes means a violation" polarity with a long list of rules, any of which it would ignore if the rules were short. Not tried: a short rule subset in the criterion slot (the 15 categories that fired in the refusals), a larger quant, the yes-token logprob as a score.

## S-w09-explorer-w09-c-5
Time per call dominated the plan more than accuracy did: 4 s per call for the 20B model on a short criterion and 15 s under the rules, so 300 allows per arm for it is 20 to 75 minutes. Two models loaded at once slowed safeguard from 4 s to 16 to 35 s per call (one contended partial run, set aside), so the local judge cannot share the GPU with another model or with a competing job; the machine also had another project's long job running in part of the window (visible in `ps`; its load was not measured). A per-call latency figure from wave 8 (2.3 s) therefore is not the figure for the real calls (4.1 s p50 with 318 median prompt tokens).
