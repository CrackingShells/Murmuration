## S-w02-explorer-w02-c-8
At USD 0.05 a worker's first request alone is 0.55 to 0.81 of B (the context load of about 13,000 prompt tokens is written to the cache at USD 2.5 per million, about 0.03). A budget below that cannot give a note, only a stop, and a stop cannot bring the fraction under 1.2. The B3 level (0.40) gave fractions 0.22 to 0.60, still no note. Somewhere between 0.05 and 0.40 there is a band where a note can land; I do not know where, and the plugin documents neither a floor nor a band. The same figure bears on the PI: it sets `Budget:` lines by role (1.5 to 3 USD) from nothing in the files.
