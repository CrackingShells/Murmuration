## S-w05-explorer-w05-b-4
The PI waits for workers for 48% of wave-4 wall time (22 waits, mean 114 s) and a further 937 s wait is the longest gap; the model itself is at most 9% of wall time. In those waits the PI session does nothing. Yet my share of "peer wake-ups" says 24 of 114 requests were started by a worker message, each reading about 240k cached tokens, 5 of them only to acknowledge. Is the number of hand-back messages (one per worker, plus SendMessage replies) the right unit, or would fewer, later wake-ups (collect hand-backs, wake once) cut requests without slowing the critical path? I could not measure this from the PI transcript alone: it needs the workers' finish times against the PI's next action.

`mcp__ccd_turn__wrap_up` (the app's "next step" box) precedes three of the four over-an-hour cache expiries in waves 1-3 (requests 305, 355, 364), at 4419, 5073 and 5429 s. Nothing in the plugin looks at this; I do not know whether a scheduled wake-up (`ScheduleWakeup` is in the tool list) keeps the cache, or what a wake-up request would itself cost.
