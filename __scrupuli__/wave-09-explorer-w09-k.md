## S-w09-explorer-w09-k-3
In wave 8 not one hand-back arrived while the PI was mid-turn (0 of 36), and the PI turn gap from about 5 to 13 h has no turn at all, ending in a human prompt. Yet explorer-w09-d counts 13 turns whose notice or message made no request (queued behind other input) and the w09d parser has no turn for the 3 queued escalation hand-backs. So "wake" as the metric's unit is tied to how this platform delivers messages: a hand-back absorbed into a running turn or delivered as a queued attachment is not a wake here. How many hand-backs are invisible to the metric in waves 1-8, and do they open files differently (explorer-w09-h found 1 of 3 queued escalations read)? The 226 arrivals against 223 wake rows (peers included) suggest few, but queued attachments are a different record type and are not in either count.
