## S-w08-explorer-w08-c-3
All 10 author and reproducer misses sit after `SubagentHandback`: the worker waits to be resumed. The waiting time (median 161 s over 22 author hand-backs, 7 over 300 s, one 4,187 s) is set by the resumer (verifier or PI), not by the worker, so it is a quantity the lab controls, and shortening it is a different lever from pricing it. But the entry that must survive the pause was written before the worker decided to hand back, so a per-phase TTL cannot be aimed at this phase by the worker itself; I found no platform control either. What would a hand-back look like if the worker's last request could be a 1-hour write (for example a dedicated final-report step)? I did not test it.
